# Visual direction — HYBRID

Keep the real map. Dress the lamps and the shop fronts.

Keyline walks OSM tiles. That is the city. Do not replace the basemap with a painted one. The work is the objects that sit on it: lanterns, awnings, the desk, the title folio lamp. One language of brass, soot glass, and warm wick — not a new world.

## Why hybrid

- The tiles already tell the ward. Painting over them fights the walk.
- The lantern is the verb. Players read it more than the street name.
- Facades (print shop, HQ plate, title folio) are the few surfaces that can take craft without breaking Leaflet.

## Art tasks (Pass 2+)

1. **Lantern glass** — Layer the globe: soot rim, warm wick, a short bloom on the map marker. Hero lantern in the vault plate must match the street marker, same cap and post. No magenta, no flat yellow circle.
2. **Shop / HQ facade** — Brass awning and a hanging lamp over the outfitter. HQ Ledger plate gets the same brass edge and a small wick so the desk feels like a storefront, not a settings sheet.
3. **Title folio lamp** — The title-screen lantern uses the in-world lantern markup (cap, frame, glass, post). Same glow as a white street lamp. The folio stays a map block, not a poster.

Map tiles stay. Lanterns and facades move first.
